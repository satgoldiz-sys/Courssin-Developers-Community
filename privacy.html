Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 13, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Policy explains how CCD collects, uses, stores, protects, and discloses personal information when you use our website, attend events, or join our community. By using the website or joining CCD, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Personal information

  • Full name, email address, and phone number
  • Country, state, city, and occupation
  • GitHub, LinkedIn, and portfolio links
  • Programming languages, technology interests, and event registrations

Account information

Account identifiers, password credentials managed by our authentication provider, and login activity. CCD does not receive or store your password in readable form.

Technical information

Depending on hosting, security, and service-provider logs, we may receive IP address, browser and device information, operating system, pages visited, and time spent on pages.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage member accounts.
  • Register members for events and programs.
  • Send relevant newsletters, announcements, and event information.
  • Recommend programs and improve our website.
  • Respond to enquiries and maintain community security.
  • Prevent fraud and abuse and produce aggregated statistics.

3. Email Communication

We may send event reminders, announcements, workshop invitations, mentorship updates, newsletters, and security notifications. You may unsubscribe from promotional emails at any time. Essential account and security messages may continue.

4. How We Protect Your Information

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including authentication-provider password protection, secure hosting, restricted administrative access, and security monitoring. No internet service is completely secure, but we work to reduce risk.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that operate CCD services, where required by law, to protect community safety and security, or when necessary for a community project.

6. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to your information, correction of inaccurate information, account deletion, withdrawal of marketing consent, a copy of your personal data, or objection to certain processing. Contact CCD to make a request; we may need to verify your identity.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our policies.

8. Children's Privacy

Members under 18 should obtain parental or guardian permission before registering or participating.

9. Third-Party Services

Our website may integrate or link to GitHub, Google, Zoom, YouTube, Discord, LinkedIn, and other services. Each service has its own privacy policy and practices. For browser storage and cookies, see our Cookie Policy.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy periodically. Updates will be posted here with a revised Effective Date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us through our official website contact page: https://ccd-gilt.vercel.app/.
