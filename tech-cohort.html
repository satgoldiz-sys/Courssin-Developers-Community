Flagship Developer Program

CCD TECH COHORT

CONNECT. COLLABORATE. CREATE. Every six months, CCD hosts a major technology gathering that brings the developer community together for hands-on learning, project showcases, career conversations, and meaningful connections.

A Milestone for the CCD Developer Community

The Tech Cohort is CCD’s flagship program, uniting developers for coding workshops, live demos, networking, mentorship, and recognition across each six-month batch cycle.

What Happens at the Tech Cohort

  • Hands-on web development and software engineering workshops.
  • Cybersecurity, AI, and emerging technology sessions.
  • UI/UX design, product, and creative tech presentations.
  • Live software demos and group project exhibitions.
  • Developer networking and technology career discussions.
  • Coding competitions, awards, and community recognition.
  • Guest speakers and practical learning opportunities.

Organized by the Community, for the Community

The CCD Transition & Delegation Programme empowers committed outgoing members to help organize the next Tech Cohort, serving as organizers, technical leads, event coordinators, publicity teams, registration coordinators, and more.

Cohort management, committees, delegates, registration, attendance, awards, and reports are supported through CCD’s admin and platform modules as configured.
