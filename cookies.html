What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They can help a site remember preferences, support performance, and provide certain features.

These technologies support core website functions such as secure login, session management, and security. They cannot be switched off where they are required for a service you request.

These may help us understand site performance or remember preferences. We will describe optional technologies here if CCD enables them.

Analytics cookies

Where non-essential cookies are enabled, we may present a banner so you can accept or reject them. You can also control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect sign-in or other website functionality.
