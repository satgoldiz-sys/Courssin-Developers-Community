Community guidelines

Mentorship Policy

Guidance for safe, respectful, and useful learning relationships within CCD.

Purpose

CCD mentorship supports learning, practical skill-building, and professional growth. Mentorship is guidance and peer learning; it is not a promise of employment, income, certification, or a specific result.

Mentor commitments

  • Treat mentees with respect and provide constructive, honest feedback.
  • Set realistic expectations about availability and areas of experience.
  • Keep personal information shared in mentoring conversations private, except where disclosure is needed for safety, a legal obligation, or reporting serious misconduct.
  • Disclose conflicts of interest and avoid using the relationship to solicit money, labour, or personal favours.
  • Refer questions outside your expertise to suitable resources rather than presenting guesses as professional advice.

Mentee commitments

  • Attend agreed sessions or give reasonable notice when plans change.
  • Ask questions, share goals, and take responsibility for practice and follow-up.
  • Respect the mentor's time, boundaries, and feedback.
  • Do not record, publish, or redistribute private mentoring conversations without everyone’s permission.

Boundaries and safety

Mentoring must remain professional and voluntary. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, romantic or sexual pressure, requests for passwords, and exploitative conduct are not acceptable. Members under 18 should involve a parent or guardian in arrangements where appropriate and should not meet an adult mentor alone in a private physical location.

Concerns and changes

A mentor or mentee may pause or end an arrangement. Report concerns or request a change through an official CCD contact channel. CCD may review reports and take appropriate action under its Terms and Conditions and Code of Conduct.

No guaranteed outcome

Mentors share experience to help learning; they do not act as a substitute for legal, medical, financial, or other licensed professional advice. Outcomes depend on each participant’s circumstances and effort.
