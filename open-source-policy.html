Community guidelines

Open Source Contribution Policy

Practical expectations for contributing to CCD projects and other open source work through the community.

Before contributing

  • Read the repository’s README, contribution guide, license, and code of conduct.
  • Check that the project accepts contributions and that your proposed work fits its goals.
  • Ask before starting large or potentially breaking changes.
  • Contribute only work you created or are authorized to submit, and identify third-party code or assets with their required licenses and attribution.

Secure and responsible contributions

  • Never commit passwords, API keys, access tokens, private member information, or production data.
  • Do not add malware, undisclosed tracking, or code that bypasses security controls.
  • Use test data, follow project security guidance, and report vulnerabilities privately to the project maintainer rather than publishing an exploit.
  • Do not upload content that violates copyright, privacy, or another person's rights.

Working with the team

Use the project's issue and pull request process, describe changes clearly, link related discussions, and respond respectfully to review feedback. Review and acceptance are at the discretion of the project maintainers. A contribution is not guaranteed to be merged, credited in a particular way, or compensated.

Ownership and licensing

You keep ownership of your original contributions. By submitting a contribution, you confirm you have the right to submit it and agree that it may be distributed under the project's stated license. Do not submit work under incompatible or unclear licensing terms.

CCD community conduct

All contributors must follow the CCD Terms and Conditions and the applicable project's code of conduct. CCD may remove content or restrict community access where these rules are violated.

Questions

For help choosing a project or raising a contribution concern, contact CCD through the Contact page.
