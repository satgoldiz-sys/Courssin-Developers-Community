Rewards & Recognition

Celebrate the Developers Who Show Up and Give Back

CCD recognizes real contributions, coding, collaboration, mentorship, leadership, and community impact. Rewards and opportunities are based on verified participation, eligibility, and availability, so recognition always reflects meaningful effort.

Custom CCD T-Shirts

Community merchandise for active members and standout contributors who help the developer community thrive.

Tech Event Tickets

Free or discounted access to developer events, conferences, and learning opportunities when available.

Certificates & Credentials

Recognition for completion, leadership, mentorship, and meaningful contributions to collaborative projects and programs.

Community Recognition

Public appreciation for consistency, teamwork, and the kind of impact that makes a developer community stronger.

Mentorship Opportunities

Pathways for experienced members to guide others, share knowledge, and grow as leaders in the tech community.

Learning Resources

Access to selected developer resources and opportunities that support practical skill-building and career growth.

How CCD points work (preview): Members can earn points for verified contributions such as completing tasks (10), finishing early (5 bonus), helping another developer (5), successful team projects (20), challenge participation (10), mentoring a beginner (15), and outstanding community impact (25). Awards are approved by authorized reviewers or administrators.
