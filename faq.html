Help centre

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about joining and taking part in Community of Courssin Developers.

Membership

Who can join CCD?

Students, beginner and experienced developers, designers, technology professionals, entrepreneurs, and anyone interested in learning technology can apply.

Where is CCD starting?

CCD is starting in Ibadan, Nigeria, with a long-term vision to grow into a global developer community.

How do I register for a batch?

Create an account, verify your email if requested, sign in, and open My Membership to apply to an open batch. Students select their institution from the available list.

How do I join the WhatsApp community?

After a successful batch registration or approval, the community invite is shown in your membership area when the invite is configured. It is also delivered through notifications when available.

Events

How do I find event details or register?

Visit the Events page, open an event card to view its details, and follow the registration information shown for that event.

Can event details change?

CCD may update event venues, schedules, speakers, or program details when necessary. Check the event page and official CCD communications for current information.

Projects and learning

Can members contribute to projects?

Members can participate in collaborative projects when opportunities are available. Follow project instructions, respect teammates, and contribute only work you own or have permission to use. Read our Open Source Contribution Policy.

Does CCD provide mentorship?

Mentorship opportunities depend on current programs and mentor availability. See the Mentorship Policy. Mentorship does not guarantee a job, internship, certification, or specific outcome.

Will I receive a certificate?

A certificate is provided only when a specific CCD program or event states that it offers one. Check that program's information and completion requirements; participation alone does not guarantee certification.

Fees and support

Does CCD charge membership or event fees?

Any fee will be stated on the relevant program or event information before you register. Do not send payments to personal accounts unless an official CCD channel explicitly confirms the payment details.

Are payments refundable?

Unless a program states otherwise, event registration fees are non-refundable after the event has started. Refund requests are considered case by case, and payment-provider fees may not be refundable. See our Terms and Conditions.

How can I contact CCD?

Use the Contact page or an official CCD communication channel published on this website.
