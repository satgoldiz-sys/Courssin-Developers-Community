Community guidelines

Event Participation & Photography/Media Release Policy

How to take part in CCD events and how event photos and videos may be used.

Event participation

Participants must follow event instructions, venue rules, applicable safety guidance, and the CCD Code of Conduct. Treat attendees, speakers, volunteers, organisers, and venue staff respectfully. Follow any registration, accessibility, age, or participation requirements listed on the event page.

Photography and recording at events

CCD may take photographs, video, or audio at in-person and virtual events. These recordings may include the event space, group activities, presentations, and attendees. CCD may use selected material on its website, social media, newsletters, reports, and other community promotional or educational channels.

Your choice and privacy

We will make a reasonable effort to tell attendees when photography or recording is taking place. If you do not want to appear in promotional photos or video, tell the event organiser before or during the event. Organisers can explain available options, such as an area outside the camera frame or avoiding identifiable close-up images where practical. You may still be visible incidentally in wide crowd or background shots.

For virtual events, you can keep your camera off unless participation requires it and the event details say so. Do not record or publish another attendee’s image, voice, or contribution without their permission.

Requests about existing media

If a CCD-controlled photo or video identifies you and you would like it removed or not used in future promotions, contact CCD through the Contact page and include the event and a description or link to the media. We will review reasonable requests and take practical steps regarding future use. We may not be able to recall material already printed, shared by others, or included in archived event records.

Minors

Participants under 18 should have parent or guardian permission to attend. A parent or guardian may contact the organiser about photography preferences. CCD will take additional care with identifiable promotional media featuring minors.

Questions

This policy works alongside CCD’s Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions. For questions or a media request, contact an official CCD organiser.
